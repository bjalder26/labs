  8. What is the correct name for HClO4?


    9. Explanation

    Acids are divided into hydracids and oxyacids. Hydracids do not contain oxygen and are named using the pattern hydro + root + ic acid (HBr = hydrobromic acid). Oxyacids contain oxygen and are named from their polyatomic ions. If the ion ends in -ate, the acid name ends in -ic acid (nitrate → nitric acid). If the ion ends in -ite, the acid name ends in -ous acid (sulfite → sulfurous acid). Do not use the prefix hydro- when naming oxyacids.

    15. Explanation

    A homogeneous mixture has a uniform composition throughout the sample. The components are mixed evenly, so one portion of the mixture has the same composition as any other portion. A heterogeneous mixture has a nonuniform composition, meaning different regions of the mixture contain different amounts of the components. Salt water and air are common examples of homogeneous mixtures, while sand and water and vegetable soup are common examples of heterogeneous mixtures.

  20. What element is defined by the following information?

    p+ = 12
    n° = 12
    e- = 12

    21. The element is


    Explanation

    The identity of an element is determined solely by its number of protons. The number of protons is called the atomic number. Changing the number of neutrons creates a different isotope of the same element, while changing the number of electrons creates an ion. Regardless of the numbers of neutrons or electrons, if two atoms have the same number of protons, they are the same element.
