    Use these steps to guide your reasoning:
    • Determine the molecular shape.
    • Determine whether the bond dipoles cancel.
    • If all bond dipoles cancel, the molecule is nonpolar.
    • If the bond dipoles do not cancel, the molecule is polar.

    • The electron-domain geometry is octahedral.
